## 6. Oncologist – endocrine therapy planning

You are the oncologist. Plan endocrine therapy after the planned surgery in May 2016.

1. What is the hormone receptor status and what does it imply for endocrine therapy?

3. Are there any cardiovascular risk factors relevant for aromatase inhibitor use?

## 7. Ophthalmologist – follow-up visit

You are the ophthalmologist seeing the patient for a follow-up in spring 2016.

1. What was the documented visual complaint and when did it occur?
2. Is there a follow-up recommendation from the neurology or internal medicine team?
3. What vascular risk factors are documented that might affect ocular health?

## 8. Ward nurse – admission assessment

You are the ward nurse completing the admission assessment on 11 April 2016.

1. What medications does the patient take at home?
2. Are any allergies documented?
3. What is her weight and BMI on admission?

## 9. Pharmacist – medication review

You are the hospital pharmacist. Review the discharge medication from April 2016 for interactions and completeness.

1. Which drugs are on the current medication list as of discharge (April 2016)?
2. Are there any known drug–drug interactions (e.g. metformin + contrast, ASA + anticoagulants)?
3. Are there any dose adjustments needed based on kidney function?

## 10. Radiologist – pre-contrast MRI safety check

You are the radiologist. The patient is referred for a contrast-enhanced MRI.

1. Is there a documented contrast media allergy?
2. Is kidney function documented (eGFR / creatinine)?
3. Is the patient taking metformin, and has it been paused?
